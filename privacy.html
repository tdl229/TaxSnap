We do not knowingly collect or process any Special Categories of Personal Data (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic data, biometric data, or health data). DATA SET / PERMISSION PURPOSE AND OPERATIONS LAWFUL BASIS & STORAGE
Local Expense Ledger, Receipt Images & Mileage Logs
